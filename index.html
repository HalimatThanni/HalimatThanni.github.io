About Me

Every Dataset Has a Story. I help businesses understand it.

Hi, I'm Halimat Sanusi, a Data Analyst with a background in digital marketing and a passion for uncovering the stories hidden within data.

I help businesses move beyond numbers by transforming raw data into meaningful insights, interactive dashboards, and practical recommendations that support smarter decision-making. Whether it's improving sales performance, evaluating operational efficiency, or identifying business opportunities, I enjoy turning complex datasets into clear, actionable solutions.

My work combines analytical thinking, business curiosity, and effective communication because I believe the true value of data or any analysis isn't the dashboard itself but the decisions it empowers.

With experience in Power BI, SQL, and data modeling, I specialize in developing comprehensive reports that reveal hidden patterns and trends in data. This portfolio showcases my projects across various sectors.
